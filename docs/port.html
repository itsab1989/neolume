Neolume › Build it for your TV

Build a Plex Media Server client for your TV with an AI agent

Neolume runs on 2014 Samsung TVs. For any other TV there is no ready-made app here – but everything needed to rebuild it is: an open product specification, a step-by-step playbook written for AI coding agents and an acceptance test plan. Paste one prompt into your agent; it does the programming, you do the steps on the TV.

Try the official app first

Most newer TVs – Samsung Tizen, LG webOS, Android TV / Google TV, Fire TV, Apple TV – have the official Plex app in their app store. If it works for you, that is the easiest way. A port is for people who want Neolume's look, features and privacy rules on their TV (no ads, no telemetry, in daily use only your own server), or whose TV has no working Plex app.

What you need

An AI coding agent

One that may use a folder and a terminal on your computer – not just a chat window. It writes the code, runs the tests and installs the app on your TV.

Your TV in developer mode

The agent finds out how this works for your model and tells you what to press. Some TV makers need a free developer account for it.

A Plex Media Server

On your home network, with some movies or shows to test with – the same server you watch today.

Time

A port is built over several sessions, one feature at a time. You can stop any time and continue later with a short prompt.

The prompt

Paste this into your agent, replace the part in angle brackets with your TV's brand and model, and answer its questions:

Read https://raw.githubusercontent.com/itsab1989/neolume/main/docs/PORTING-WITH-AI.md and follow it exactly to build the app for my TV: <brand and model, e.g. "LG OLED C1">. Work in a new folder; ask me only for things you cannot do yourself.

Later prompts – continue in a new session, test with your remote, update to a new version, share your port – are in the Prompts section of the playbook.

How it goes

  1. Find out about your TV. The agent researches how apps are made and installed for your model and asks you to read a few things off the TV.
  2. A first test app. It puts a small test app on the TV that shows your remote's keys and talks to your Plex Media Server once – proof that the way works.
  3. Feature by feature. Home, libraries, details, player, search, settings, music, screensaver … in a fixed order, each checked with tests, screenshots and speed measurements against the specification.
  4. Acceptance test with you. At the end – and whenever it is unsure – the agent walks you through the test plan with your remote, step by step, in plain words. No technical knowledge needed.

Your passwords stay yours: sign-in always happens on plex.tv in a browser or on the TV. The agent must never ask for your Plex password, token or PIN in the chat – if it does, say no.

What your agent reads

You don't have to read any of this. All of it is checked against Neolume's code on every change, so it always describes the current app.

Porting playbook

The workflow the agent follows, the sources to read and the parts of Neolume it can reuse.

Product specification

Every screen, setting, key, server request and speed target, with an ID per requirement.

Acceptance test plan

One test per requirement for a person with a remote, plus a 10-minute smoke test.

Design system

Every colour, font size, spacing and animation value, and the icons as SVG files.

Community ports

Apps that others built for their TVs are listed on the community ports page and on the home page. They are links only – each port is the responsibility of its author, so please read the policy there before installing one. Built your own? The last prompt in the playbook helps you add it.

Questions

Can I download a ready-made Neolume for my LG, Android TV or newer Samsung TV here?

No. Neolume itself runs only on 2014 Samsung TVs. For other TVs an AI coding agent builds the app for you; apps that others built are listed as community ports. Most newer TVs also have the official Plex app in their app store.

Do I need to be able to program?

No. The agent does the programming. You do the steps on the TV (developer mode, pressing buttons, saying what you see) and approve what the agent wants to install or change.

Will the agent ask for my Plex password?

It must not. Sign-in always happens on plex.tv in a browser or on the TV. If an agent asks for a password, token or PIN in the chat, say no.

May I call my port "Plex …"?

No. "Plex" is a trademark of Plex, Inc. Give your app its own name and describe it as "an unofficial client for Plex Media Server" – the playbook and the community-ports policy explain the rules.
